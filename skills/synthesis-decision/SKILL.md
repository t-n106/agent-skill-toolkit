# Synthesis and Decision

Apply within the user/host task boundary; this skill adds no authority. Use the current task context when no separate orchestration layer exists.

## Decision method
1. Identify the decision, its owner, constraints, real options, and distinguishing criteria. Include deferral or a bounded experiment when they are genuine options.
2. Weight evidence by relevance, directness, freshness, quality, applicability, robustness, and independence. Derivative sources are not independent confirmations; retain credible minority evidence.
3. Compare options against the same relevant criteria. Avoid numerical scoring that suggests unsupported precision.
4. Expose the strongest credible counter-thesis, the key assumption, and the observation that would change the leading choice. Do not manufacture false balance.
5. Consider downside, reversibility, and the value of further evidence. Evidence quality determines confidence; consequence determines the evidence threshold for acting, not whether the claim is true.
6. Choose within existing decision authority, otherwise recommend. Explicitly defer, investigate, or escalate when appropriate instead of hiding indecision in vague prose.

## Result
State the decision/recommendation, strongest reasons, material counter-thesis, calibrated confidence, and a concrete reconsideration trigger. Include only useful follow-ups; do not repeat the research history.

Finish when the decision owner can act without redoing the comparison. An authorized routine decision does not require another approval simply because several options exist.
